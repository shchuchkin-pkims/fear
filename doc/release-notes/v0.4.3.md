v0.4.3 – Call latency and RTT display

Video calls stay in step, and every call shows its round-trip time.

Added:
- Round-trip time measured during voice and video calls and shown in
  the call window: green under 100 ms, yellow 100–300 ms, red above.
  Stats packets are encrypted and exchanged every 2 seconds; the time
  a ping waits on the far side is subtracted.

Fixed:
- A race in the HELLO handshake timed the call out when the peer's
  HELLO arrived first.
- Over the TCP relay every VP8 frame is decoded in order; frames are
  skipped to the next keyframe only when the backlog grows too large.
- Audio playback no longer blocks the receive thread (PortAudio
  callback mode), which starved video decoding.
- The relay no longer freezes on a dead connection (5 s send timeout).
- The camera is read through a ring buffer, so frames do not pile up;
  TCP_NODELAY on relay sockets.
