v0.5.0 – Handles, contacts and personal chats

The relay gains state: registered handles, an encrypted contact list
per user, and personal chats that work without the other side online.
Completes Phase B of the roadmap.

Added:
- Handles in the form nickname@server, bound to an Ed25519 key: one key
  has one handle per server. Registration is offered on first connect,
  and a restored identity finds its handle again by its key.
- An encrypted contact list kept on the relay and synchronised; its key
  is derived from the identity key, so the relay cannot read it.
- Personal chats under a key derived from both identities (X25519):
  opening one does not need the other person online.
- Encrypted identity backup as a .fbk file or a QR code, importable on
  desktop and Android.
- Local message history with full-text search; a peer profile dialog;
  removing a contact or a room.
- The connect mode is chosen automatically: an empty room is created
  with a fresh key, a populated one is joined by key exchange.
- Application keep-alive: the client pings at least once a minute, and
  the relay closes a connection silent for 4 minutes.
- A relay server image for amd64 and arm64:
  ghcr.io/shchuchkin-pkims/fear-server:v0.5.0.

Changed:
- One sidebar for contacts and groups; light and dark themes that
  follow the system.
- The default room is "general" instead of "guest".

Known limitations:
- No forward secrecy: disclosure of an identity key exposes the history
  of personal chats and rooms. Scheduled for Phase C.
- The identity file on Linux and Windows is not encrypted by the
  operating system.
- The release archives hold only fear and fear_gui: calls and the
  updater are missing. Fixed in v0.6.0.
