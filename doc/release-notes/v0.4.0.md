v0.4.0 – Video calls

Encrypted video calls.

Added:
- video_call: VP8 video (FFmpeg and libvpx) shown with SDL3, every
  fragment encrypted with AES-256-GCM, fragmented and reassembled over
  UDP.
- Quality presets – low 320x240 at 15 fps, medium 640x480 at 25 fps,
  high 1280x720 at 30 fps – with adaptive bitrate.
- A peer that goes silent for 5 seconds is noticed, and a returning one
  is picked up automatically.
- Receive-only mode for a machine without a camera.
- Camera and quality selection in the graphical client.

Downloads: fear-linux-x86_64.zip, fear-windows-x86_64.zip.
